NIKOS combines entry control, announcements and monitoring of the mobile power supply into one platform – exactly right amid noise, crowds and mobile infrastructure.

Catering and showmen's businesses often connect their equipment to the temporary power distributors uncoordinatedly – phase overloads and outages are the result. With , load and consumption data at the distributors can in future be measured continuously. If a phase approaches the set limit, this is shown in the system and an alarm is raised before the fuse trips. If a circuit fails anyway, this is shown immediately on the and optionally sent by text message to the radio of the responsible staff member. continues to run uninterrupted on battery power during a power failure.

Vorteil: Überlastungen und Stromausfälle werden erkannt, bevor sie den Betrieb stören. Benefit: Overloads and outages are detected before they disrupt operations.

Fluchtwege, Parkplätze und Dunkle Ecken brauchen zuverlässige Beleuchtung. Kabellose Notlichter werden in Minuten ohne Werkzeug an Masten, Bäumen oder Traversen montiert, laden tagsüber über ihr Solarpanel und schalten per Dämmerungssensor automatisch. Eine Demontage ist nur mit Spezialwerkzeug möglich (Diebstahlschutz).

Escape routes, car parks and dark corners need reliable lighting. Wireless emergency lights are mounted in minutes without tools on masts, trees or trusses, charge during the day via their solar panel and switch automatically via a twilight sensor. Removal is only possible with a special tool (theft protection).

Vorteil: Wartungsfreie, kabellose Beleuchtung – in Minuten montiert. Benefit: Maintenance-free, wireless lighting – mounted in minutes.
